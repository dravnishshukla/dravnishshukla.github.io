I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay**, specializing in Air Quality, Exposure Science, and Environmental Health. My work bridges the gap between environmental monitoring and public health risk analysis.

Currently, I am leading research on the vertical distribution of ambient air pollution in high-rise residential buildings, integrating field monitoring with indoor-outdoor exposure assessment. My core expertise includes PM2.5 chemical characterization, atmospheric pollution modeling, and computational health-risk analysis.

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering at the **Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT)**, where my thesis focused on a comprehensive study of traffic noise impact on students' cognitive learning and teachers' well-being in roadside schools. 

My research has been published in leading peer-reviewed journals including *Environmental Research*, *Applied Acoustics*, and *Ergonomics*, and I regularly present my findings at international conferences such as INTERNOISE and the Transportation Research Board (TRB) Annual Meeting.
